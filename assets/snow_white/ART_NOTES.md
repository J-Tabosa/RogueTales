## Prompt

Create a clean production 2D game animation spritesheet matching the supplied angular cel-shaded anime/cartoon reference. Transparent RGBA background, NO scenery, NO floor, NO cast shadows, NO text or grid. EXACTLY 3 columns and 4 rows, twelve isolated full-body drawings, reading order left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Every cell has the SAME character size, same feet baseline, same body pivot at 42% cell width, facing RIGHT in 3/4 side-view for a side-scrolling mobile RPG. Body height about 75% cell height, with wide transparent gutters so NO sprites or weapons touch neighboring drawings or cell edges. Preserve outfit consistently. First row: frame1 calm standing eyes open, frame2 subtle weight shift eyes open, frame3 eyes half closed. Second row: frame4 standing eyes CLOSED for a blink, frame5 walking right left foot forward, frame6 walking passing pose. Third row: frame7 walking right right foot forward, frame8 walking opposite passing pose, frame9 attack windup. Fourth row: frame10 attack release/slash toward RIGHT, frame11 attack follow-through, frame12 return to ready. Exactly ONE character per cell, both boots fully visible. Attack weapon stays entirely inside its wide cell. Consistent head/body proportions, face, costume and colors, crisp readable silhouettes. Sheet aspect ratio 1:1 (3 wide columns, 4 shorter rows), preferably high resolution 2048x2048. Use ONLY SNOW WHITE on LEFT of reference: adult pale woman, short wavy BLACK bob hair, large RED hair bow, blue puff sleeves with red inserts, white high collar, fitted blue bodice, black gold-buckled waist belt and delicate gold chain, YELLOW high-low skirt with red ornaments, white ruffled underskirt, RED cape, chunky BLACK buckle boots. She holds a shiny RED APPLE. Attack winds back with apple then throws the apple to the RIGHT; include apple next to her outstretched hand at release, NOT a bomb or sword. Keep long skirt slightly lifted at front so boots and alternating steps are visible. Do not include Mulan.
