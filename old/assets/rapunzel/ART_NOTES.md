# Arte da Rapunzel

As duas folhas foram criadas com a geração de imagens integrada (ImageGen), a partir da referência visual fornecida pelo usuário. Os PNGs gerados foram preservados sem alterações de pixels.

- `rapunzel_sheet.png`: 1122 × 1402, 4 colunas e 5 linhas; o jogo usa os quatro primeiros desenhos para idle/piscada e os oito seguintes para caminhada.
- `rapunzel_attack.png`: 1086 × 1448, 2 colunas e 4 linhas; oito poses de ataque de cabelo.
- `rapunzel_frames.tres`: regiões isoladas por contornos em `MeshTexture`, com tamanho visual normalizado e origem nos pés. O canvas da Rapunzel mede 1000 × 600 e o pivô fica em (360, 580), com espaço à direita para o cabelo.
- `scenes/heroes/rapunzel.tscn`: modelo editável, nome, tipo de ataque e ponto de impacto.

## Prompt da folha geral

Create a production game spritesheet based on ONLY the blonde Rapunzel on the LEFT of the reference image. Use her exact angular anime/cartoon art style, adult appearance, huge golden hair, green eyes, purple corset dress with short white underskirt, purple puff sleeves, black belt, black fingerless gloves and black chunky strapped boots. DO NOT include the witch or the scenery. Transparent RGBA background. Layout is an EXACT 4-column by 5-row grid, 20 equal-sized cells, ordered left to right then top to bottom, wide enough margins that no character or hair crosses a cell edge. Full body always, standing on the same baseline at the bottom of each cell, identical character size, hip position and camera in every frame, facing RIGHT in a 3/4 side view appropriate to a side-scrolling game. Each cell contains ONE Rapunzel only. No text, labels, numbers, grid lines, floor, shadows, or decorations. Keep the entire character, both boots and long hair inside each cell. The head-to-foot body height is consistent across all frames. First row (4 frames): calm standing idle, slight weight shift, half blink, both eyes closed blink. Rows 2 and 3 (8 frames): a coherent full walking cycle to the RIGHT, alternating legs and arms, shoulders counter-rotate, long hair trailing gently LEFT, frame 8 returns toward frame 1. Rows 4 and 5 (8 frames): attack by using golden HAIR as a whip, NO sword NO gun NO frying pan. Frame 1 ready, frame 2 leaning back winding up hair, frame 3 turning shoulders hair curls back LEFT, frame 4 hair swinging forward, frame 5 hair lashes forcefully to the RIGHT, frame 6 fully extended golden hair whip to the RIGHT with dynamic curved strands, frame 7 recoiling and retracting hair, frame 8 returning to calm ready stance. Keep body at consistent horizontal pivot, hair can expand to the RIGHT within the cell; allow room in every cell for the attack. Crisp outlines, cel shading and the same sophisticated gold/purple/black palette as the reference. The sheet should be 4:5 aspect ratio, ideally 2048x2560 with cells 512x512.

## Prompt da folha de ataque

Use Rapunzel from this spritesheet as the exact character reference: same adult blonde with extremely long gold hair, purple corset dress and puff sleeves, white short underskirt, black belt, gloves and chunky strapped black boots, sharp cel-shaded cartoon/anime lineart. Create ONLY a clean 8-frame HAIR-WHIP ATTACK animation spritesheet on true transparent background. Layout: EXACTLY TWO COLUMNS and FOUR ROWS, 8 separate rectangular cells. Image ratio 3:4, ideally 1536x2048, each cell 768px wide x 512px tall. Each cell must contain one whole full-body character, feet on the same baseline, same body size and same body pivot x within the cell. Character faces RIGHT. The wide cells provide enough space for the hair weapon. Leave a CLEAR EMPTY TRANSPARENT GUTTER of at least 40px between all characters, hair strands and each neighboring cell. No strand, glove, boot or effect may cross the cell boundary or touch another sprite. Keep body at 40% of cell width in every frame, head-to-foot height roughly 80% of cell height. Eight poses in reading order: 1 calm combat ready; 2 prepare leaning back and gathering hair; 3 shoulder twist winding gold hair back to left; 4 start swinging hair forward; 5 strong rightward strike, hair is a thick golden whip curving to the RIGHT; 6 fully extended rightward golden hair lash, bending in an S shape so it fits within the cell, entire body and weapon inside the cell; 7 recoil and retract hair; 8 return to ready pose. IMPORTANT: exactly 8 separate full-body sprites, never overlapping, clear consistent grid. No duplicate people inside a cell. No witch, no weapons besides HAIR, no scene, no text, no grid lines, no numbers, no floor or cast shadows. Maintain identical outfit and hair color, especially matching the reference's black boots and white skirt frills.
