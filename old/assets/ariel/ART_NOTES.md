# Arte da Ariel

Modo: ImageGen integrado, derivação da referência enviada. Figurino adaptado para túnica roxa opaca. O PNG gerado foi preservado sem alterar pixels.

- `ariel_sheet.png`: 1254 × 1254, três colunas por quatro linhas, 12 desenhos.
- `ariel_frames.tres`: quatro quadros de idle/piscada, quatro de deslocamento flutuando e quatro de tridente. Leitura por linhas: idle 1/2/2/1, blink 1/3/4/1, walk 5/6/7/6, attack 8/9/10/11 (índices aqui começam em 1).
- Recortes nativos MeshTexture isolam contornos. Canvas 1000 × 600, origem (360, 580), altura visual normalizada; pivô horizontal estabilizado para evitar saltos laterais.
- Cena `scenes/heroes/ariel.tscn`: cauda, cabelo e ombros acompanham os passos de nado; ataque acerta aos 48% da duração editável.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: wholesome fantasy RPG game animation spritesheet. Use image as reference for colors, hairstyle and accessories only. Two adult heroines appear in reference; generate ONLY the requested one. Design a family-friendly adult fantasy heroine in a modest fully covered adventuring costume, neutral non-suggestive heroic poses. Sharp clean cartoon cel shading. True transparent background. Layout exactly THREE COLUMNS by FOUR ROWS, twelve separate full-body sprites facing RIGHT. Identical body size, pivot and feet/tail baseline in every cell. Huge empty transparent gutters between rows and columns; complete body, accessories and weapon inside each cell. NO scenery, floor, shadows, text or grid. Reading order: 1 standing idle eyes open; 2 slight weight shift; 3 half blink; 4 eyes closed blink; 5 locomotion left phase; 6 passing phase; 7 opposite phase; 8 opposite passing phase; 9 attack preparation; 10 attack action toward RIGHT; 11 follow-through; 12 return to ready. Four idle drawings, four locomotion drawings and four attack drawings. Exactly one character per cell. Square sheet, high resolution. ONLY ARIEL, right heroine: adult red-haired mermaid guardian with green eyes, gold seashell earrings, emerald scaled fish tail and mint split fins, ornate gold trident. Her torso is FULLY COVERED by an opaque PURPLE LONG-SLEEVE TUNIC with a small gold seashell clasp at collar; no bare midriff. Long flowing red hair. Always has a FISH TAIL with no human legs. Upright floating pose. Locomotion is four gentle swimming tail-undulation phases, torso steady and shoulders slightly counter-rotate. Attack is gold trident thrust RIGHT with a small stylized turquoise water swirl: windup, extend, follow-through, recover. Entire trident and tail stay inside cell. No blonde princess.
